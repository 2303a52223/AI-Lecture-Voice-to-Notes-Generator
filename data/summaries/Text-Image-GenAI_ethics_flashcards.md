# Flashcards — Text-Image-GenAI (Ethics)

- Q: Name three ethical risks of text→image models.
  A: Misinformation/deepfakes, bias/stereotyping, privacy leaks.

- Q: One technical mitigation for detecting synthetic images?
  A: Watermarking or provenance metadata.

- Q: Why is dataset curation important?
  A: Reduces bias, improves provenance, and lowers privacy risks.
